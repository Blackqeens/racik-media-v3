# RACIK MEDIA V3 - LOGISTICS & PROVENANCE RULES

## 1. LOGISTICS FACILITATION BOUNDARIES

In accordance with the _Logistics Principle_ established in `system-context.md` and `logistics-provenance.md`, Racik Media acts strictly as an ecosystem orchestrator and trust facilitator.

- **No Asset Ownership**: The platform does not own, rent, or operate physical warehouses, fulfillment hubs, trucks, or delivery fleets.
- **Fulfillment Autonomy**: Sellers and Distributors are fully responsible for physical stock packaging, custody, and choosing the routing protocol (integrated third-party systems, seller-managed delivery, or buyer pickup).

## 2. DUAL-CHANNEL LOGISTICS WORKFLOW

Logistics tracking behavior splits logically based on the marketplace transaction channel to match the scale, operational requirements, and complexity of the trade.

### Retail Channel (B2C / C2C)

- **Workflow**: Driven by automated status synchronization with designated third-party logistics networks.
- **State Coupling**: The transition of an order from locked to transit states inside the transaction framework programmatically requires a verified and valid tracking identifier generated by the fulfillment system.
- **Delivery Validation**: Final release of funds depends on verifiable delivery confirmations cross-referenced with user receipt indicators.

### B2B Wholesale Channel (Institutional Shipping)

- **Workflow**: Driven by custom contract negotiations and milestone-based multi-stage shipping pipelines.
- **Logistics Flexibility**: Supports complex shipping protocols including freight cargo, customized local shipping pools, split-deliveries, or Buyer institutional pickup arrangements.
- **Validation Gates**: Payout delivery milestones require verified confirmation logs, handover certificates, or regional check-in records agreed upon during the contract negotiation state.

## 3. TRACEABILITY PRINCIPLE

Whenever practical, products within the ecosystem must be traceable to their structural origins.

- **Origin Context**: Traceability criteria encompass granular information regarding the Producer, Region, Community, Production Method, and specific Origin Batch.
- **Ecosystem Value**: Traceability is enforced because it actively strengthens community trust, protects product authenticity, and preserves local socioeconomic value.

## 4. PROVENANCE TRACKING

Logistics at Racik Media is not merely about moving packages; it is the physical proof of the _Item Story Rule_.

- **Narrative Tracking**: The delivery infrastructure facilitates the physical verification of a product's batch movement from its registered local source node to the final consumer node.
- **Authenticity Assurance**: Physical verification layers exist to map the movement of local heritage goods, stamping chronological timestamps at every major handover intersection to validate the product's origin story.

## 5. AUTHENTICITY PRINCIPLE

The platform strictly promotes authentic products and truthful origin claims, protecting local artisans from mass-market exploitation.

- **Prohibited Falsification**: Participants are strictly prohibited from falsifying product origins, artisan identities, production methods, geographic indications, or certification claims.
- **Spillover Impact**: Authenticity violations are treated directly as severe critical trust violations, triggering the _Trust Spillover Protocol_ across the master identity.

## 6. SUPPLY CHAIN TRANSPARENCY

Participants are expected to provide accurate, honest, and real-time information regarding their internal supply capabilities.

- **Data Integrity**: Accurate data must be maintained regarding stock availability, actual production status, order fulfillment readiness, and shipping progress.
- **Misrepresentation Penalties**: Deliberate misrepresentation of supply chain information to bypass market rules or manipulate buyers will result in immediate moderation review, structural reputation penalties, or listing suppression.

## 7. RESPONSIBLE FULFILLMENT

Participants are bound to the absolute accountability of execution once a commercial commitment is entered.

- **Operational Expectations**: Sellers, Distributors, and ServiceProviders are expected to fulfill transactions in a timely, accurate, and transparent manner.
- **Enforcement Parameters**: Repeated fulfillment failures, unjustified logistical delays, or systemic abandonment of confirmed orders will trigger manual moderation reviews, direct multi-dimensional reputation penalties, and immediate role-specific commercial restrictions.

## 8. LOGISTICS DISPUTES & FRAUD PROTECTION

- **Predatory Tracking Lockout**: The input of fake, recycled, or manipulated tracking identifiers to artificially bypass escrow hold states is dynamically blocked. Verified infractions trigger immediate critical role restrictions.
- **Chain of Custody Disputes**: If a fulfillment sequence fails within the designated agreement window or damages a provenance-tracked item, the transaction parameters enter a locked dispute state.
- **Arbitration Framework**: The Moderator Panel evaluates the documented chain of custody timeline and verification records to determine operational liability before authorizing any partial or full refund workflows.

## 9. ENVIRONMENTAL RESPONSIBILITY

In alignment with the platform's long-term sustainability goals, participants are structurally encouraged to minimize their ecological footprint.

- **Sustainable Practice**: The ecosystem promotes the adoption of sustainable practices in packaging, transportation efficiency, regional storage optimization, and low-impact material handling.
- **Platform Recognition**: The platform may recognize sustainable logistics initiatives and eco-friendly supply paths through trust indicators, specific profile badges, or community recognition programs.

## 10. LOCAL PRODUCER RECOGNITION

The platform formally recognizes producers, artisans, farmers, and local communities as the authentic originators of all ecosystem value.

- **Attribution Guardrails**: Whenever practical, product provenance mappings must preserve, safeguard, and proudly display clear attribution to the original creator.
- **Socioeconomic Integrity**: Recognition of product origin loops serves directly to strengthen decentralized marketplace trust, institutional fairness, and regional cultural preservation.

## 11. PROVENANCE BEFORE PROMOTION

Ecosystem visibility is governed by origin transparency, not promotional or capital spend.

- **Visibility Multiplier**: Products and services holding verified, complete provenance mappings are prioritized for enhanced visibility and search index ranking over items with hidden or incomplete origin information.
- **Strategic Ranking Signal**: Trust, transparency, and traceability take absolute priority over raw promotional or discount intensity. Verified item stories are treated as a high-priority ranking signal within the platform's search and discovery systems.

## 12. AUDIT TRAIL LOGGING

Every physical movement milestone, delivery validation, and shipping status exception must leave an unalterable digital footprint logged securely in `logistics_change_logs` (plural, per `database-conventions.md`).

- **Required Fields**: `order_id`, `tracking_record_id`, `current_status` (e.g., `manifested`, `in_transit`, `delivered`, `carrier_exception`, `tracking_invalid`), `correlation_id`, and `created_at`. Status values use `lowercase_snake_case` (ADR-001 D7). Sensitive data (resi number, recipient, address) is stored encrypted in the Logistics domain owner; events carry only `tracking_record_id` as reference.
